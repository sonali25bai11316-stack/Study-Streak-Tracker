**Study Streak Tracker – Project Statement**

**1\. Problem Statement**

Students often study regularly but may not keep track of their study time, consistency, and progress. Without proper tracking, it can be difficult to maintain a regular study routine and achieve weekly study goals.

The Study Streak Tracker is a beginner-friendly console-based Java application designed to help students record their daily study sessions, monitor their study streaks, view study statistics, and set weekly study goals.

**2\. Scope of the Project**

The project focuses on providing a simple and easy-to-use study tracking system through a console-based Java application.

The system allows users to:

\- Record their study sessions.  
\- Track their daily study consistency.  
\- Calculate and maintain study streaks.  
\- Set and monitor weekly study goals.  
\- View study statistics and progress.  
\- Receive achievement/progress information based on their study activity.

The project is developed using standard Java and object-oriented programming concepts without requiring a website, external database, or complex framework.

**3\. Target Users**

The primary target users are:

\- College students  
\- School students  
\- Students preparing for examinations  
\- Learners who want to maintain a consistent study routine

**4\. High-Level Features**

The major features of the Study Streak Tracker include:

1\. **Study Session Tracking**   
   Users can record their study sessions and study duration.

2\. **Study Streak Tracking**    
   The application tracks consecutive study activity to help users maintain consistency.

3\. **Weekly Study Goals**    
   Users can set study goals for the week and monitor their progress.

4\. **Study Statistics**    
   Users can view information about their study activity and overall progress.

5\. **Achievement Tracking**    
   The system provides achievement/progress information based on the user's study activity.

6\. **Console-Based User Interface**    
   The application provides a simple menu-driven console interface for interacting with the system.

**5\. Technologies Used**

\- Java  
\- Object-Oriented Programming (OOP)  
\- Standard Java Libraries  
\- Console-based interface
